\section*{Approved reduced-block APE cleaning}

The synchronized raw channels are partitioned into non-overlapping 0.05 s
records with physical midpoint times $t_k$.  For the cavity channel,
\begin{equation}
\bar V_k = \operatorname{mean}(V_{\rm trans}),
\qquad
V_{\min,k}=\min(V_{\rm trans}).
\end{equation}
The centered 60 s local cavity reference is the linearly interpolated 90th
percentile of eligible block averages,
\begin{equation}
V_{{\rm ref},k}=
Q_{0.90}\left(
\{\bar V_j:\ |t_j-t_k|\le30\,{\rm s}\}
\right),
\end{equation}
and the cavity decision is
\begin{equation}
M_{{\rm cav},k}=
\left[V_{\min,k}\ge0.90\,V_{{\rm ref},k}\right].
\end{equation}
The reference is recomputed from provisionally good records three times, as in
the earlier validated APE cavity-quality workflow.

For each reduced record,
\begin{equation}
R_{2f,k}=\sqrt{\bar X_{2f,k}^{,2}+\bar Y_{2f,k}^{,2}},
\qquad
I_{0,k}=C_{\rm trans}\bar V_k,
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\eta_{0,k}=
\sqrt{
\frac{2\sqrt{2}\,R_{2f,k}}
{H_2qGI_{0,k}}
}.
\end{equation}
Only cavity-good records are eligible to define the centered 60 s eta0
reference,
\begin{equation}
\eta_{{\rm ref},k}=
Q_{0.90}\left(
\{\eta_{0,j}:\ |t_j-t_k|\le30\,{\rm s},
\ M_{{\rm cav},j}=1\}
\right).
\end{equation}
The eta0 decision is
\begin{equation}
M_{\eta,k}=
M_{{\rm cav},k}\land
\left[\eta_{0,k}\ge0.90\,\eta_{{\rm ref},k}\right],
\end{equation}
with the eta0 reference likewise recomputed from provisionally good records
three times.  The final mask is
\begin{equation}
M_{{\rm final},k}=M_{{\rm cav},k}\land M_{\eta,k}.
\end{equation}
The historical fixed cut $\eta_0\ge0.019$ is not used in this approved path.
Every raw sample inherits the quality state of its reduced record.  No
interpolation, smoothing, fallback reference, gap filling, deletion, or
concatenation across rejected records is performed.

\section*{Approved APE raw-to-calibrated chunk conversion}

The recorded demodulated quadratures are treated as RMS voltages. For each
validated raw sample,
\begin{equation}
I_0 = C_{\mathrm{trans}} V_{\mathrm{trans}},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
R_{2f}=\sqrt{X_{2f}^2+Y_{2f}^2},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\eta_0 =
\sqrt{
\frac{2\sqrt{2}\,R_{2f}}
{H_2 q G I_0}
},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\rho_X =
\frac{X_{1f}}
{\sqrt{2}\,qGI_0\eta_0},
\qquad
\rho_Y =
\frac{Y_{1f}}
{\sqrt{2}\,qGI_0\eta_0}.
\end{equation}
Here $H_2$ is the measured photodiode/electronics response at $2f$.
The science quadratures remain separate. Invalid physical samples preserve
their original indices and calibrated outputs are set to NaN. This stage
performs no smoothing, filtering, interpolation, resampling, clipping, phase
correction, gain-imbalance correction, leakage correction, or X/Y combination.

\section*{Approved C-LPSD bridge}
For a selected uninterrupted segment of $N$ samples at rate $f_s$, the
diagnostic one-cycle frequency is $f_{1/T}=f_s/N$. The C feasibility lower
limit is
\begin{equation}
f_{\min}^{\rm C}=\frac{\mathrm{SBIN}}{T}
\left[1+(1-O/100)(m-1)\right],
\qquad
f_{\max}<f_s/2,
\qquad
J_{\rm des}=\left\lceil
\frac{\log(N/2)}{\log(1+1/Q)}
\right\rceil,
\quad Q=10^6.
\end{equation}
The C executable is invoked in LPSD mode with Kaiser window $-w\,-2$, PSLL
200 dB, 50\% overlap, linear-regression detrending ($-r\,1$), desired averages
100, and minimum averages 1. It reports a one-sided PSD $S_x(f)$ directly; the
bridge applies no scale beyond $-x\,1$ and defines
$\mathrm{ASD}_x=\sqrt{S_x}$. For the approved $m=1$,
$f_{\min}^{\rm C}=\mathrm{SBIN}/T$. SBIN is queried from the actual
configured C executable, never hard-coded. This is a window feasibility limit,
not a data-quality cut. For input unit $U$, $[S_x]=U^2/\mathrm{Hz}$ and
$[\mathrm{ASD}_x]=U/\sqrt{\mathrm{Hz}}$.

\section*{Legacy calibrated quadrature review}
The older review-only path uses a frozen reference denominator
\begin{equation}
D[n]=\sqrt{2}\,I_0[p(n)]\,\eta_0[p(n)]\,R\,G,
\qquad
X_{\rm ell}[n]=X_V[n]/D[n],
\qquad
Y_{\rm ell}[n]=Y_V[n]/D[n].
\end{equation}
This legacy path is retained for historical cross-checks and is not the
authoritative implementation of the new raw-to-calibrated streaming stage.
